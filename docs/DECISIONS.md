# Research decisions and evidence boundaries

1. User authorized a complete restart, implementation, training, evaluation, paper writing, and saving models to ronitrajput-beep/ML-project. Earlier fallback files remain outside this checkout.
2. Scholarly hosts remained proxy-blocked. GitHub and raw GitHub worked. Full-text evidence was subsequently obtained lawfully from publisher-maintained PMLR repositories, including separate newer `main/assets` PDF storage. No paywall bypass was used.
3. Chose imbalanced maintenance classification because two public tables were immediately retrievable, CPU training was feasible, and the required metrics are meaningful. Interest in maintenance alone was not the selection criterion. APS is real but anonymized; AI4I is synthetic. Neither experiment establishes prospective failure lead time.
4. Rejected claims that missingness shift, source masking, feature deletion, or missingness avoidance are new. DAMS, TabFSBench, MINTY and MA learning directly establish prior work. The chosen contribution is the controlled training/threshold/transfer comparison and released evidence, not a verified novel algorithm.
5. Fixed the protocol before fitting, hashed it into every split manifest, and retained the originally selected configurations. No retuning after viewing test results.
6. Main models: logistic, HGB, Group-HGB; Cell-HGB is a deliberately cheap control. Every HGB uses identical fixed learner settings. No neural network was added for appearance. No claims of reproducing exact published implementations or beating published SOTA.
7. Split before augmentation; all preprocessing/ranking is training-only. Only validation labels choose a threshold. The official APS test is retained. Group4/5 are omitted from deliberate group augmentation and threshold tuning; random-cell control can still mask their individual cells, as intended for the transfer question.
8. Main results are mixed. APS benefit is small and its conditional interval includes zero. AI4I anticipated-outage improvement does not transfer to speed loss. Keep these results in the abstract, results, conclusion, README and final response.
9. Bootstrap scopes differ: APS jointly resamples its shared test rows across seeds; AI4I uses seed 11 only. Do not attach the AI4I first-seed interval to its three-split mean or treat repeated seeds as independent datasets.
10. Training-only redundancy audit revealed highly correlated APS counters. A joint-outage sensitivity was declared separately and run without retraining. It remains explicitly exploratory.
11. F1-selected thresholds have worse APS diagnostic cost than the .5 operating point. An F1 improvement is not a claim of business value or reduced breakdown costs.
12. Ten PDFs were retrieved. Review is targeted at main-text sections plus relevant EGG-GAE supplement, not a claim to fully audit all proofs/appendices or exhaustively cover 2026. Related unrefereed GitHub projects are discovery evidence only, not sources of quantitative manuscript claims.
13. Author identity and affiliation were not inferred from repository ownership or Git configuration. The manuscript uses a generic course-project author line. Actual authors should insert their details and review the manuscript before submission.
14. Writing passes applied No AI Slop, Human Prose and Write Like Me principles: concrete claims, connected paragraphs, no inflated significance, no invented evidence or detector-bypass claim. Scientific limits were preserved.

15. A representation-count concern motivated a declared post-hoc clean-repeat control (six extra fits, no primary-model replacement). AI4I clean-repeat primary F1 .614 exceeds group masking .601. The paper therefore does not attribute the original gain specifically to masking.
