# Manuscript writing audit

Applied the installed No AI Slop, Human Prose, and Write Like Me instructions to the scientific manuscript. This is an editorial pass, not a detector-bypass claim or a claim to match a personal writing style.

What changed / checked:

- Use measured results and concrete mechanisms in the abstract and conclusion.
- Preserve uncertainty: APS interval includes zero; group masking fails on a held-out outage; a clean-repeat control prevents a mask-specific benefit claim.
- Keep original research claims separate from cited source-paper results, theory assumptions, exploratory checks, and future hypotheses.
- Use connected technical paragraphs and remove generic importance claims, canned transitions, decorative contrast, and promotional novelty language.
- Keep all nine requested content sections. Abstract and References follow normal IEEE formatting rather than forcing section numbers onto them.
- Do not invent authors, affiliations, experiments, datasets, quotations, paper metrics or proof verification.
- Do not describe scores from balanced loss as calibrated probabilities or recorded-condition classification as a validated forecast of future failures.

The final author/supervisor review remains necessary before academic submission. Editorial fluency does not establish novelty or scientific correctness.
