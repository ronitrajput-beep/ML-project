# Targeted full-text literature review

Review date: 26 October 2026. Ten peer-reviewed papers were retrieved as full PDFs from PMLR's publisher-maintained GitHub repositories. The review used relevant main-text methods, evaluation and discussion sections, plus the EGG-GAE accuracy table in its supplement. Proofs and every appendix were not exhaustively checked. This is not an exhaustive literature census or a verified worldwide novelty search. No abstract-only paper is used for a substantive manuscript claim.

`sources.json` pins publisher metadata and PDF storage to Git commits. `corpus.json` records hashes and page counts. `scripts/retrieve_literature.py` reproduces acquisition. Physical PDF page numbers are used below. Original paper results are not comparable to the new APS/AI4I runs because datasets, tasks, splits and endpoints differ.

## Synthesis and falsification

1. Reconstruction quality, missingness reliance, score ranking, thresholded classification, and operational cost are different endpoints. The first two are prominent in several reviewed papers; conflating them would produce an unsupported gap claim.
2. Assumptions and transfer matter. Zhou et al. report a real-data failure of adjustment; Stempfle et al. explicitly identify changing missingness as a limitation. These findings support retaining a source baseline and testing an outage family excluded from deliberate training corruption.
3. Importance-ranked feature deletion is not new: TabFSBench directly falsifies that novelty claim. Source masking is also not new: Zhou et al. directly falsify that claim. Missingness-avoiding ensembles are not new either.
4. No paper in this selected ten-paper corpus contains the literal strings AI4I or Scania in its extracted full text. That lexical observation is reproducible but **does not prove that the exact protocol or problem is novel worldwide**, including differently named data or other publications.
5. GitHub discovery additionally found `cdhuangjin/missingness-shift-minority` and `kovariati/MissingnessShiftBench`, both describing broad missingness-shift evaluation. These are related software artifacts, not treated as peer-reviewed evidence or as sources for quantitative manuscript claims. They further weaken broad novelty claims.

## Bounded contribution

The resulting paper is an original empirical case study: two imbalanced maintenance tables, a training-by-threshold separation, a matched selected-cell corruption control, and transfer to two deliberately untrained full-group outages. Its contribution is the auditable comparison and measured failure boundary. It does not claim a new ML algorithm, a universal advantage, an exhaustive 2026 review, or publication acceptance.
