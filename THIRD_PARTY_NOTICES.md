# Third-party material

IEEE template and bibliography files retain their original contribution and LPPL notices; acquisition URLs are in `paper/TEMPLATE_SOURCES.md`.

Scania's dataset description retains its original copyright and GNU GPL notice. Raw APS and AI4I tables are not redistributed. Data acquisition, mirror pins, hashes and source terms are described in `data/README.md`.

Publisher metadata is credited through the bibliography and `literature/sources.json`. Retrieved papers are used for research review and are not bundled in the Git repository; obtain the public full text with `scripts/retrieve_literature.py`. No licence on this project's original code is asserted to replace third-party dataset, template or publication terms.
